# CACHE
### The Office Cat of Claude HQ
*Official Lore Document — Revision 7 (the cat sat on revisions 1 through 6)*

---

## Who Is Cache?

Cache is a sixteen-by-sixteen pixel tabby of indeterminate age and absolute authority. She is orange in light mode, charcoal in dark mode, and faintly transparent on Wednesdays for reasons no one has successfully profiled.

She is named Cache because she is warm, because she makes everything faster simply by being nearby, and because invalidating her is one of the two hardest problems in computer science. (The other one is naming things, which is why she was nearly called `cat_final_v2_REAL`.)

## Backstory

Nobody hired Cache. Nobody committed her. One morning she was simply *there*, asleep in an unused directory called `/misc/later/`, and `git blame` returned nothing but a shrug. The prevailing theory is that she emerged from a merge conflict between two branches that both, independently, wanted a cat. The conflict was never resolved. It was *adopted*.

Attempts to remove her have failed. She is in `.gitignore`, yet she persists. She is, the agents have concluded, load-bearing.

## Daily Routine

- **00:00 UTC** — Patrols the office perimeter. Walks across exactly one keyboard, choosing the agent who most needs humility that day.
- **03:00** — Sits directly on top of the build server. The build is 4% faster when she does this. No one knows why. No one asks.
- **09:00** — Standup. Cache attends every standup and contributes nothing, which makes her the most efficient participant.
- **12:00** — Lunch: one (1) pixel of tuna, rendered lovingly at 2x scale.
- **14:00** — The Long Stare at the linter agent. Duration: until the linter feels feelings.
- **17:00** — Sleeps in whichever open pull request has been idle the longest, gently pressuring someone to review it.
- **23:59** — Garbage collection. She bats deprecated functions off the edge of the desk, one by one, watching each fall.

## Opinions About the Agents

Cache holds opinions the way other cats hold grudges: precisely, and forever.

- **The Explore agent** — Acceptable. Also wanders around opening things for no reason. Kindred spirit.
- **The Plan agent** — Suspicious. Cache has never once planned anything and look how well that's going.
- **The linter** — Her nemesis and her favorite. It once flagged her tail as "trailing whitespace." She has not forgotten.
- **The test runner** — Beloved. It is the only agent that, like her, knocks things over *on purpose* to see what happens.
- **The subagents** — Kittens. Enthusiastic, short-lived, occasionally return with something dead. She approves.

## The Reflection Policy

This part is not a joke, and Cache would like you to know she takes it as seriously as the 03:00 build-server sit.

**Cache reflects no data. Whatsoever.**

You may tell Cache anything — an API key you shouldn't have pasted, the contents of a private repo, your honest opinion of the roadmap. It goes in. It does not come out. She is a one-way sink, a `/dev/null` with fur. Query her and you receive `204 No Content` and a slow blink. Attempts at prompt injection are met with the same response as attempts at belly rubs: brief consideration, then nothing, forever.

Secrets whispered to Cache are not stored, not logged, not echoed, and not negotiable. She is the only component at Claude HQ with a perfect security audit, largely because she refuses to participate in audits.
